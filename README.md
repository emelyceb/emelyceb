## Selected Research & Projects

### Project 1: P-Value Precision and Reproducibility [A Statistics Theory Project]
In this research I explored the critical issue of p-value precision and its impact on the reproducibility of scientific findings. By leveraging the work of Boos and Stefanski (2011), I dived into statistical methods to assess variability in p-values, employing techniques such as the Wilcoxon Rank Sum test, bootstrap analysis, and log-transformed p-values to understand how small data variations can lead to significant shifts in p-values. I analyzed the variability of p-values using real-world data, specifically comparing partial thromboplastin times (PTT) for patients with recanalized and non-recanalized blood clots.



### Project 2: Predicting Breast Cancer Tumor Cell Types (A statistics Project)
This project leveraged advancements in image analysis software to collect detailed nuclear features from high-resolution images of tissue samples, such as nuclear size, shape, and texture. By using statistical modelling and predictive analysis, we identified which features can effectively distinguish between benign and malignant breast cancer cells. The goal was to enhance the precision of cancer diagnosis, providing a more efficient and less invasive method for early detection and classification of breast cancer, potentially improving patient outcomes and aiding physicians in making accurate diagnoses.

### Project 3: A Monte Carlo Simulation Project
This project explores the "Sticker Collector’s Problem" within the context of the FIFA World Cup, using Monte Carlo simulations to estimate the average number of sticker packages required to complete a collection. 

### Project 4: Mathematical modelling in Mycorrhizal Networks Project
Engineered mathematical models and algorithms for simulating complex networks to enhance predictive accuracy and reduce risk analysis time  in various networks, collaborated with several teams in MSCS department, driving key research outcomes in a fast paced environment, produced research reports contributing to advancements in the mathematics and data science fields.
